Minesweeper in Python (Scratch-built)

While this may not be the most technically impressive or unique project to highlight, it still holds a special place
in my heart as one of my first completed projects I felt proud enough to share with others, and vastly deepened my
understanding of programming. To complete this project I had to design and create my own custom render pipeline,
independently discovering the idea of 2D arrays. Utilizing multiple of them, each could representi a different "layer"
to hold different information such as bomb locations, distance numbers, revealed tiles, and flag locations.
Finally all were fed into a single system to "overlap" them and compile the result into the final "screen" layer
which was printed out. While this aproach may have took up more data than needed in hindsight, I still appreciate how
it allowed for most of the computation to be done at game start, and left the actual gameplay very lightweight.
